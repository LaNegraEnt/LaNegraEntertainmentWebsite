Technical requirements

Technical rider

These are the minimum technical and logistical facilities for a La Negra Entertainment performance. The organiser provides them; they are required for the performance to go ahead.

Download PDFSalsa Brass Band rider (PDF)See also article 7 of our terms

Sound

  • Microphones for solo instruments, conductor, etc.
  • Enough speakers for the audience
  • Sound system suited to the number of guests

Salsa Brass Band

  • 7 microphones for trumpets / trombones
  • 1 microphone for the singer
  • 2 overheads for the drummers
  • 1 monitor for the piano
  • 1 bass amplifier for the bass guitar

Lighting

  • Basic stage lighting
  • Extra lighting for effects (optional)
  • Good visibility for artists and audience

Stage

  • Solid stage area with room for all band members
  • Size depending on the line-up
  • Stage riser if needed
  • Stable, level floor without obstacles

Indoors or outdoors

  • Outdoors, always a covered area (tent or canopy)
  • All members must be able to stand and play in the dry
  • Moisture damages instruments, woodwind and brass
  • Expensive equipment must be protected too

Dressing room

  • A lockable dressing room for the artists
  • Suitable for changing before the performance
  • A safe place for personal belongings
  • If possible: separate rooms for women and men

Food & drinks

  • Water and soft drinks for all band members and crew
  • Available before and during the performance
  • Snacks for band members and crew
  • For long performances: a hot meal or catering

Transport & logistics

  • Parking as close to the venue as possible
  • Reserved spaces for all band members' vehicles
  • Easy access for moving instruments
  • Loading area kept free until after the performance

Power

  • Sufficient power outlets near the stage
  • At least 2 grounded sockets
  • Enough capacity for sound and lighting
  • Safe cabling, no cables across walkways

Important: if these facilities are missing, the performance may be delayed or cannot go ahead. Any costs arising from this are borne by the organising party. In doubt or have questions? Please contact us in good time.
