What to expect

The Salsa Brass Band combines the power of a brass band with bass guitar, piano and a singer. The result: live salsa and Latin music that gets your guests out of their seats and fills the dance floor.

You can also book the regular Brass Band and the Salsa Brass Band together: for example one Brass Band set and one Salsa Brass Band set. That line-up comes with an adjusted rider.

  • Live music: brass band, bass guitar, piano and vocals
  • Perfect for weddings, corporate events, festivals and theme parties
  • Can be combined with samba dancers or a salsa workshop

The difference from a regular brass band

A regular brass band is mainly percussion and horns. The Salsa Brass Band adds piano, bass guitar and a singer. That makes the sound fuller and warmer, and ready for a real stage or dance moment. Not just a brass band, but a big band experience.
