What to expect

Choose a salsa workshop, a percussion workshop or a tailor-made combination package.

A workshop is ideal as an interactive break, team building or part of a larger programme. Guests take part, laugh together and get into the mood in an accessible way. We tailor it to the group, the venue and the time available.

  • Salsa workshop: the basic steps with professional dance instructors, for all levels
  • Percussion workshop: Caribbean rhythms on congas and bongos
  • Combination package: several workshops, or a workshop plus a live act

More than watching: this is experiencing

With a workshop your guests don't just watch, they take part. That creates connection, energy and a memory that lasts much longer than a performance alone. Short, powerful and for everyone.
