What to expect

Live steel pan music instantly brings a sunny Caribbean atmosphere to your reception, drinks, dinner, festival, wedding or corporate party. Calm and stylish in the background, or festive and interactive as a tropical eye-catcher.

The sound of the steel pan is recognisable, warm and instantly atmospheric. Perfect for moments when guests arrive relaxed, linger and feel the tropical vibe straight away.

  • Tropical welcome at an entrance, drinks or opening
  • Indoors and outdoors, small and large

  • Can be combined with samba dancers or workshops

Why a steel band works

Tropical welcome: ideal for an entrance, drinks, reception or opening. Flexible: indoors and outdoors, small and large, as background music or a show moment. Premium atmosphere: stylish live music that suits corporate events, weddings and festivals.
