About us

La Negra Entertainment from Rotterdam brings Caribbean and Latin entertainment to parties and events throughout the Netherlands and beyond. From samba dancers and brass bands to live Latin music, workshops and a photo booth.

With over 30 years of experience and 1000+ events, we know exactly how to bring a party to life. You tell us your plans, we put the programme together and make sure everything is right on the day.
