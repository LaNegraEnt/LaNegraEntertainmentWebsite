Article 2

Definitions

  • La Negra Entertainment: the entertainment agency that offers and books acts, artists and services.
  • Client: the party (person, company or organisation) that places a booking with La Negra Entertainment.
  • Artist / act: the performers engaged by La Negra Entertainment, such as dancers, musicians, fire breathers or workshop instructors.
  • Agreement: the booking or contract between La Negra Entertainment and the client.
  • Performance: the execution of the agreed act(s) at the agreed venue and date.
  • Rider: the technical and logistical requirements the client must provide for the performance.
Article 3

Applicability

These terms and conditions apply to all quotes, bookings and agreements of La Negra Entertainment. By placing a booking or signing an agreement, the client declares that they accept these terms.

Deviations from these terms are only valid if expressly agreed in writing. The client's own terms and conditions are expressly rejected.

Article 4

Quotes & bookings

All quotes from La Negra Entertainment are without obligation and valid for 14 days from the date of issue, unless stated otherwise.

A booking is final after:

  • written confirmation by La Negra Entertainment, and
  • receipt of the deposit (see article 8).

La Negra Entertainment reserves the right to refuse a booking without stating reasons.

Article 5

Formation of the agreement

The agreement is formed at the moment La Negra Entertainment confirms the booking in writing and the deposit has been received. As long as the deposit has not been paid, no rights can be derived from the booking.

La Negra Entertainment will inform the client in good time about the acts, the length of the performance and any technical requirements (rider).

Article 6

The performance

La Negra Entertainment will perform the agreed acts to the best of its ability and at a professional level. Quality and punctuality are central.

The performance takes place on the agreed date, time and venue. In case of delays due to circumstances beyond the control of La Negra Entertainment (e.g. traffic, force majeure), we will contact the client as soon as possible.

Important: La Negra Entertainment reserves the right to interrupt or end the performance early if the client does not meet its obligations, the safety of the artists is at risk, or the venue does not meet the rider requirements.

Payment obligation in case of interruption: in all cases of interruption or early termination for the reasons above, the client remains liable for the full agreed amount (100%), regardless of how much of the performance took place. No refund will be given. Interruption or termination of the performance due to the actions or negligence of the client never releases the client from its payment obligation.

Article 7

Obligations of the client

The client is responsible for providing, in good time and in full:

  • a suitable, safe venue with enough space for the acts;
  • all technical requirements as described in the rider (sound, lighting, power, stage);
  • a lockable dressing room for the artists;
  • water, soft drinks and snacks for the artists and crew;
  • parking for all band members' vehicles, as close to the venue as possible;
  • all permits and exemptions required for the event.

If the above facilities are missing, the performance may be delayed or cannot go ahead. Any costs arising from this are borne by the client.

The client is responsible for music copyright fees (such as Buma/Stemra in the Netherlands or the local equivalent) for public performances.

Article 8

Payment

  • Deposit: € 50 upon confirmation of the booking.
  • Balance: the remaining amount no later than 7 days after the performance.

Payments must be made to the account number stated on the invoice. In case of late payment, La Negra Entertainment reserves the right to terminate the agreement, without prejudice to its right to compensation.

In case of late payment, statutory interest is due, plus any collection costs (max. 15% of the outstanding amount, minimum € 40).

All prices exclude travel costs and 9% VAT, unless expressly stated otherwise.

Article 9

Cancellation by the client

Cancellations must always be made in writing. The following cancellation costs apply:

More than 30 days before the event25%
Between 14 and 30 days before the event50%
Less than 14 days before the event100%

Percentages apply to the total amount. The deposit already paid is deducted from the cancellation costs.

Article 10

Cancellation by La Negra Entertainment

La Negra Entertainment has the right to cancel a booking in case of force majeure, illness of the artists or other circumstances beyond its control. In that case:

  • the client will be informed as soon as possible;
  • La Negra Entertainment will do its utmost to offer an equivalent replacement;
  • payments already received will be refunded in full if no replacement is possible.

La Negra Entertainment is not liable for any further consequential damage in case of cancellation due to force majeure.

Article 11

Prices & VAT

All prices stated exclude VAT (9%) and travel costs, unless expressly stated otherwise.

Travel costs are calculated based on the location of the event and are communicated in advance. For bookings outside the Netherlands, additional travel and accommodation costs may be charged.

La Negra Entertainment reserves the right to adjust prices in case of changes in VAT rates or government measures.

Article 12

Liability

La Negra Entertainment is not liable for damage resulting from:

  • force majeure, illness or other circumstances beyond its control;
  • the client's failure to meet the obligations in article 7;
  • damage to property of the client or third parties during the performance, unless caused by intent or gross negligence of La Negra Entertainment.

The liability of La Negra Entertainment is in all cases limited to the amount invoiced for the performance concerned.

The client is liable for damage to instruments or equipment of La Negra Entertainment caused by inadequate or unsafe conditions at the venue.

Article 13

Intellectual property & images

La Negra Entertainment retains all intellectual property rights to its acts, choreographies, musical arrangements and promotional material.

Photos and videos made by or on behalf of La Negra Entertainment during performances may be used for promotional purposes on social media, the website and other channels.

The client may share images of the performance on social media, provided La Negra Entertainment is tagged or mentioned. Commercial use of images is only permitted with written permission.

Photos and videos made by the client or guests in which the artists are recognisable may not be used commercially without permission.

Article 14

Privacy & data protection

La Negra Entertainment processes personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Client data is used exclusively for:

  • performing the agreement and invoicing;
  • communication about the booking;
  • sending relevant information about our services (with consent).

Data is not shared with third parties without the client's express consent. The client always has the right to view, change or have their data deleted via info@lanegra-entertainment.nl.

Article 15

Complaints

Complaints about a performance or service must be submitted in writing within 7 days after the event via info@lanegra-entertainment.nl or WhatsApp +31 6 25 17 80 50.

La Negra Entertainment handles complaints within 14 days and aims for a satisfactory solution by mutual agreement. We take complaints seriously and use them to improve our services.

Article 16

Disputes & governing law

All agreements with La Negra Entertainment are governed exclusively by Dutch law.

Disputes are preferably resolved by mutual agreement. If this is not possible, disputes will be submitted to the competent court in the district of Rotterdam.
